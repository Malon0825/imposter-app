## Part 2 — Filipino review

The app shows every card and every rule in English and Filipino. You're the Filipino check. Read it like a Filipino friend at a party would: on a phone, with a drink in hand.

**What to check in each Filipino line**
1. **Same meaning as the English.** Nothing added, dropped or softened, especially numbers (points, players, rounds), and who can and can't play the card.
2. **Natural, casual Tagalog**, the way friends talk (Taglish is fine where a Filipino player would say it that way, e.g. "taya", "boto", "round"). Not formal or textbook.
3. **Short.** A card face fits about two short lines. If yours is much longer than the English, tighten it.
4. **Same game words everywhere.** Keep these in English, as they are on screen: card names, Common / Rare / Golden, Imposter, Accomplice, Detective, Judge, Decoy, Gambler, Underdog, Bounty, Drink/Dare, clue, clue board, SUSTAINED / OVERRULED / BLOCKED / VETOED. Use one Filipino word per idea and stick to it (for example always "taya" for bet, "boto" for vote, "bunot" for draw).

**How to answer:** one table with only the rows you'd change:

| Card or rule | Current Filipino | Your Filipino | Why (one line) |
|---|---|---|---|

If a row is fine, leave it out. At the end, list any word choices you made that should apply across all rows.

### C. How to play (rows marked "new" are new for v11)

| Rule | English (source) | Filipino (check this) |
|---|---|---|
| Draw / Bunot | Before roles appear, you have 10 seconds to draw once or skip. Time runs out? You skip. | Bago ipakita ang mga role, may 10 segundo ka para bumunot nang isang beses o lumaktaw. Kapag naubos ang oras, awtomatiko kang lalaktaw. |
| Price / Halaga | A draw costs 3 points. Underdogs draw for free. | 3 puntos ang isang bunot. Libre ang bunot ng mga Underdog. |
| Deck / Mga baraha (new) | Cards come in three tiers: Common, Rare and Golden. There are 10 Golden cards; each leaves the deck once drawn. Used Common and Rare cards go to the bottom of the deck, so you'll see every card before any repeats. | May tatlong tier ang mga baraha: Common, Rare at Golden. May 10 Golden; hindi na ito ibinabalik kapag nabunot. Ang nagamit na Common at Rare ay ibinabalik sa ilalim ng deck, kaya lalabas muna ang lahat ng baraha bago may maulit. |
| Draw limit / Hangganan (new) | Power cards need 6 or more players. Each player can buy up to four draws per session. Free Underdog draws don't count toward that. Below 6 players, cards pause and you keep your hand. | Kailangan ng 6 o higit pang manlalaro para sa power cards. Hanggang apat na bunot ang mabibili ng bawat isa sa isang session. | Privacy / Sino ang makakakita | Everyone sees who drew. When a card is played, everyone sees who played it and its tier, but not which card. | Makikita ng lahat kung sino ang bumunot. Kapag may gumamit ng baraha, makikita ng lahat kung sino at anong tier, pero hindi kung anong baraha. |
