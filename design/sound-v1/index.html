IMPOSTER · SOUND SET V1

Sound board

Every sound is made in code with the browser's audio engine, so there are no audio files, nothing to download, and it works offline. Tap a sound to hear it, or play a whole round in order. Use headphones or a phone speaker: the low thuds and heartbeats get lost on laptop speakers.

Ready.

Shared · every phone at once

The host sets a start time and every phone plays it together, the way the results beats are timed today. They're mostly short hits, so a few milliseconds' difference between phones sounds like one bigger hit, not an echo. A phone more than about 300 ms late skips the sound.

Personal · your phone only

Short reactions to your own taps. Phones sit next to each other, so none of these may give a secret away: the draw sounds the same for every tier, and nothing depends on your role.

DELIBERATELY SILENT
"You can play a card now" (a chime would tell your neighbours you hold a card for this phase) · private reads (Private Eye, X-Ray, Master Key, Borrowed Hint, Word Count) beyond the normal tap · your role and word cards · tier-specific sounds on your own draw.

Rules

NO MUSICNo background music or loops. The only musical moment is the session-complete flourish.
SHORTPersonal sounds under a second. Shared sounds up to about 2 seconds; the vote heartbeat runs only until the last ballot locks.
NO SECRETSNo sound may reveal a tier you drew, a role, or a private read. Tier sounds only play when the tier is already public.
SETTINGSA Sound switch in settings, on by default at moderate volume. iPhone browser guests unlock sound with their first tap ("I'm ready").
