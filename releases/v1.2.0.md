# Imposter 1.2.0 — Full Cast

Download **imposter-1.2.0-android.apk** on your Android phone and open it to install. It updates Imposter 1.0.0 or 1.1.0 in place, using the same signing key, and keeps your settings.

Only the host needs the Android app. Connect everyone to the same Wi-Fi, start a party, and share its invitation QR. Android and iPhone guests can join in a browser.

## Update every Android device

This release changes the round protocol. A 1.2.0 host only accepts native guests running 1.2.0. An older app is turned away with **Update Imposter to join this party, or open the invitation in your browser.** Browser guests always load the current screens from the host, so they need no update.

## Full Cast: everyone has a role

Every round now deals a secret role to every player, from 3 to 12 players.

| Players | Roles dealt |
|---|---|
| 3 | Imposter, Decoy, Gambler |
| 4 | Imposter, Decoy, Judge, Gambler |
| 5 | Imposter, Accomplice, Detective, Judge, Decoy |
| 6+ | All five, plus a Gambler for every extra player |

- **Imposter**: gets a hint instead of the word and wins by escaping. From five players they also try to name their accomplice and the decoy.
- **Accomplice**: knows the word and the imposter, and wins with the escape. Voting for the Detective costs a point.
- **Detective**: is told one innocent player and names the accomplice alongside the imposter.
- **Judge**: a correct read pays +2, and ties go the Judge's way when the Judge caught the imposter. From six players the Judge's vote counts twice.
- **Decoy**: from five players they hold a related word and must blend in without being spotted. In smaller parties they hold the real word and play as bait: a point if anyone votes for them, nothing if the room convicts them.
- **Gambler**: bigger bets; the top bet pays +5 against a 3-point loss.

Each player sees their role on its own screen before their card. The lobby shows tonight's cast for the current headcount. Results show every player's role and outcome. Roles are dealt with private fairness weights, so everyone takes their turn at each role over a session. Reading streaks are gone, because each role now scores its own way.

## Also new since 1.1.0

- **Random round rules.** Turn them on from session settings. Classic, Act It, Sound Only, Two Truths and a Lie, If It Could Talk, and Sell It are drawn before topic voting. The draw favours the rules played least, and every rule has its own reviewed topic packs.
- **The round runs itself.** A shared rule reveal opens on every phone, topic voting follows automatically, a tied topic draws its own winner, and the cards are dealt without a tap. **Next round** starts straight from the standings.
- **Word deck.** The host remembers played words across rounds, sessions and restarts, and keeps similar words apart. Every word has an offline definition: swipe your card, or open **Word meaning** in Results.
- **One ballot, one shared reveal.** Everyone locks a single vote; the imposter's never counts. Each phone shows its own result first, then the room's verdict and a list of how everyone did. Who voted for whom and everyone's bets stay private.
- **Everyone can bet.** Every role can stake 0–3 points on its own goal, including the imposter's escape.
- **Skip a spoiled round** from the round menu. Points, pending deductions and played words carry over.
- **More topics.** The spoken rules offer 11–13 topic packs each. Relationships, Sex education, Body & puberty and Consent stay off until the host switches them on.
- **Screenshots work again** everywhere except while a private card is face up.
- **Steadier connections.** A brief Wi-Fi blip no longer sends a player to the reconnect screen, and party links shared through a group chat now join cleanly.
- **Report from Results.** Reporting is a sheet on the Results screen, and the host's **Continue** closes it.

The signed APK supports ARM64 phones and x86_64 emulators running Android 8 or later. A SHA-256 checksum is attached alongside the APK.
