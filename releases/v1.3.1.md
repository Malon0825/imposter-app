# Imposter 1.3.1

Download **imposter-1.3.1-android.apk** on your Android phone and open it to install. It updates Imposter 1.0.0 through 1.3.0 in place, using the same signing key, and keeps your settings.

## Fix

- **Browser guests can use power cards.** In 1.3.0, a player who joined in their phone's browser was turned away when they tapped Draw, with "Only the installed host app can do that." They also couldn't play a card, change their bet or answer Last Word. They can now do all of it, just like players in the app. Host controls such as Deal now still belong to the host.

## Who needs to update

- **The host phone.** It runs the game and serves the browser screens, so install 1.3.1 on it.
- **Android guests on 1.3.0** can keep playing with a 1.3.1 host. Older versions still need to update or join in the browser.
- **Browser guests** need nothing: they load the game from the host.

The signed APK supports ARM64 phones and x86_64 emulators running Android 8 or later. A SHA-256 checksum is attached alongside the APK.
