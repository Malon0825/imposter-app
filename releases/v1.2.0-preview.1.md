# Imposter 1.2.0 Preview

Download **imposter-1.2.0-android-preview.apk** on your Android phone and open it to install. Look for **Imposter Preview** in your launcher. It installs alongside Imposter 1.1.0 and starts with its own settings and word history. The existing app and its data stay on your phone.

Only the host needs the Android app. Connect everyone to the same Wi-Fi, start a party in Imposter Preview, and share its invitation QR. Android and iPhone guests can join in a browser. For native Android guest play, use the same preview build on each Android device.

## What’s new

- Turn random round rules on or off from session settings. When enabled, the app selects Classic, Act It, or Sound Only before topic voting and filters the topics for that rule.
- The host keeps track of played words across rounds, sessions, and app restarts. The deck also avoids recently played words that tend to produce similar clues.
- Each of the 581 words has an offline definition. Civilians can swipe left or tap **Swipe for meaning** on their private card. Everyone can open **Word meaning** in Results after voting.
- Reconnect fixes prevent stale timeouts and rounds getting stuck after someone leaves. Drink or Dare actions now apply to the turn the host saw.

The signed preview supports ARM64 phones and x86_64 emulators running Android 8 or later. A SHA-256 checksum is attached alongside the APK.
