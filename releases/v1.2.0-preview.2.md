# Imposter 1.2.0 Preview 2

Download **imposter-1.2.0-android-preview.apk** on your Android phone and open it to install. Look for **Imposter Preview** in your launcher. It installs alongside Imposter 1.1.0 and starts with its own settings and word history. The existing app and its data stay on your phone.

Only the host needs the Android app. Connect everyone to the same Wi-Fi, start a party in Imposter Preview, and share its invitation QR. Android and iPhone guests can join in a browser. For native Android guest play, use the same preview build on each Android device.

## Update every Android device

This preview changes the round protocol. A host running this build accepts native guests from **this preview build only**. An older installed app is turned away with **Update Imposter to join this party, or open the invitation in your browser.** Browser guests always load the current screens from the host, so they need no update.

## What’s new

- **The round runs itself.** Kickoff opens a shared rule reveal on every phone, topic voting opens on its own when the reveal ends, a tied topic draws its own winner, and the host deals the private cards without a tap. A repeat round starts straight from the standings with **Next round**.
- **The rules take turns.** The random draw now favours the rules the party has played least and halves the odds of repeating the one just played, so a single rule stops dominating a session. Every rule stays reachable.
- **Three new spoken rules.** Two Truths and a Lie, If It Could Talk, and Sell It join Classic, Act It, and Sound Only in the random draw, each with its own reviewed topic packs.
- **One ballot, one shared reveal.** Everyone locks a single suspect vote, the imposter included. Only civilian votes count, a tie lets the imposter escape, and the result opens as a shared verdict card before each player's own recap.
- **The imposter can bet.** **Bet on your escape** stakes 0–3 points on getting away, settled against the group verdict, with the perfect-bluff bonus unchanged.
- **Skip a spoiled round.** The host can end a round from the round menu and start a replacement. Points, streaks, pending deductions, and played words all carry over.
- **Report from Results.** Reporting is a sheet on the Results screen instead of a separate step, and the host's **Continue** closes it.

The signed preview supports ARM64 phones and x86_64 emulators running Android 8 or later. A SHA-256 checksum is attached alongside the APK.
