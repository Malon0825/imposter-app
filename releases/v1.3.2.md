# Imposter 1.3.2

Download **imposter-1.3.2-android.apk** on your Android phone and open it to install. It updates Imposter 1.0.0 through 1.3.1 in place, using the same signing key, and keeps your settings.

## Fixes

- **No more storage error when a new session starts.** With power cards on, the round dealt after the draw wasn't saved to the word deck. The next save then failed with "Could not save or load the word deck. Check device storage", usually at the start of the next session. Your phone's storage was never the problem. Rounds with power cards now save their words, so words also stop repeating across parties.
- **iPhone browser guests reconnect on their own.** An iPhone often reloads the page when it wakes, before Wi-Fi is back. The player was left on a screen telling them to join the host's Wi-Fi. The page now keeps looking for the host and puts the player back in their seat as soon as it answers.
- **Party links keep working after you join.** The page used to drop the invitation from its address once you joined, so a browser that lost your saved player could only say "scan the host's QR". The address now keeps the invitation, so you always see the party and can reconnect.
- **Join buttons fit small phones.** On short screens, "Continue in browser" was cut off at the bottom.
- **One name for each thing.** The standings tag for players far behind now says **Underdog**, like the rules. The cards you hold are **Your cards** everywhere, the scores screen is **Standings**, and a lost bet you can't cover sends you to **Drink or Dare**.

## Who needs to update

- **The host phone.** All fixes run on the host, which also serves the browser screens.
- **Android guests on 1.3.0 or later** can keep playing with a 1.3.2 host.
- **Browser guests** need nothing: they load the game from the host.

The signed APK supports ARM64 phones and x86_64 emulators running Android 8 or later. A SHA-256 checksum is attached alongside the APK.
