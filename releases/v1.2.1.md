# Imposter 1.2.1

Download **imposter-1.2.1-android.apk** on your Android phone and open it to install. It updates Imposter 1.0.0 through 1.2.0 in place, using the same signing key, and keeps your settings.

## Update every Android device

Scoring changed, so this release uses a new round protocol. A 1.2.1 host only accepts native guests running 1.2.1. A 1.2.0 app is turned away with **Update Imposter to join this party, or open the invitation in your browser.** Browser guests load the current screens from the host, so they need no update.

## The Accomplice is easier to play

- **Your vote counts.** The Accomplice's ballot always counted as one normal vote. Their ballot now says so: if the imposter and someone else look neck and neck, their vote on the other player can tip it so the imposter escapes.
- **No cost for voting the Detective.** The −1 for naming the Detective is gone, so the Accomplice can vote freely. Their range is now −3 to +5.

## Fixes

- Below six players, the Judge's own result no longer says "×2". Their vote only counts twice from six players.

## Behind the scenes

- New scoring tests play 1,500 random rounds at every size from 3 to 12 players and check each verdict and score against the written rules. Further tests play whole sessions to a winner.
- The browser smoke tests now cover Full Cast, and three timing races in them are fixed.

The signed APK supports ARM64 phones and x86_64 emulators running Android 8 or later. A SHA-256 checksum is attached alongside the APK.
