# Imposter 1.3.0

Download **imposter-1.3.0-android.apk** on your Android phone and open it to install. It updates Imposter 1.0.0 through 1.2.1 in place, using the same signing key, and keeps your settings.

## Update every Android device

This release adds power cards, so it uses a new round protocol. A 1.3.0 host only accepts native guests running 1.3.0. An older app is turned away with **Update Imposter to join this party, or open the invitation in your browser.** Browser guests load the current screens from the host, so they need no update.

## Power cards

With 6 or more players, the host can turn on **Power cards** in the lobby. It's off until they do.

- **Draw before roles.** Each round, everyone can draw one card for 3 points or skip. There's no timer: when you're done you go on to your role, and anyone still reading their card catches up. Up to 4 paid draws per session.
- **37 cards in three tiers.** Common, Rare and Golden, each with its own art. Golden cards are one of a kind per session, and your Golden chance grows a little with every draw that isn't Golden.
- **One card per round.** Play it when you see your role, during the discussion, or with your vote. Passive cards go off by themselves if you didn't play one.
- **Everyone sees who played a card and its tier, never which card.** When cards clash, the higher tier wins, and every phone shows the verdict: BLOCKED, SUSTAINED, OVERRULED or VETOED.
- **A way back for whoever is far behind.** From round 3, Underdogs draw free, can always bet 3, and collect a Bounty when the leader loses a round.
- **Card text for everyone.** Every ability is written in plain words, and each phone can switch the card text to Filipino.
- **The cards fit the roles.** Each card shows whether it suits your role this round. A card your role can't use says so.
- **Results replay every card played**, standings show the leaders and Underdogs, and a finished session ends with the Story of the night.

## A new look and sound

- **Case-file noir theme** on every screen: evidence tags, stamps, typewriter labels and new role art.
- **Sound effects** for taps, reveals and results. Shared moments play together on every phone, and a phone that arrives late stays quiet instead of echoing. Each phone has its own Sound switch.
- **Your role on the ballot.** The vote screen now shows your role's portrait, name and how your vote works.
- **Game motion** for draws, plays and verdicts, without slowing the round down. Reduced motion is respected.

## Fixes

- The chosen topic now stays on screen for a moment before the round starts.
- Your secret word sits in the middle of the role card.

The signed APK supports ARM64 phones and x86_64 emulators running Android 8 or later. A SHA-256 checksum is attached alongside the APK.
