# IMPOSTER

The discussion roster includes an optional public clue history, with one short clue per player per clue turn. The host can start further clue turns or open voting without requiring submissions. Roles, word, scoring, and imposter-selection history stay unchanged across clue turns. Players may edit/remove their own recorded clues until voting; voting shows the full read-only history. Host memory preserves it across reconnects and clears it for the next game round. See design/clue-board-v2/README.md.

Android-first local social deduction game, built with Tauri 2, Rust and React. iOS is a later target. Current game mechanics, exactly as coded, live in MECHANICS.md; PLAN.md keeps the original brainstorm; word catalog lives in TOPICS.md. No accounts, cloud party service, discussion timers, AI dares, or role rotation. One imposter selected with private fairness weights each round always gets an indirect starting hint. Everyone votes privately; every award comes from the player's role, optional bets settle from the original ballot, and escaping imposters earn +2 plus +1 for a perfect bluff. A tie at the top lets the imposter escape unless the Judge's ballot breaks it toward the imposter; there is no runoff. Human-given dares, sequential punishment turns, and session rankings race to a host-selected target of 10, 15, 20, 25, or 30 points. See MECHANICS.md for complete mechanics.

The user approved design/proposal-v1/*.png and requested faithful implementation with animations. This is implementation authorization, not an open visual exploration. Do not replace the approved design with generated alternatives.

After results, each player can privately report several other players or skip on a dedicated screen. Missing submissions and skips count as no reports. A strict majority of the full round roster schedules −1 point after the following completed round's normal scoring; all deductions stop at zero. Reports reset each round, and the host can continue without waiting. Reporting never changes Drink/Dare eligibility. See PLAN.md for timing, privacy, and reconnection rules.

Before every round, all participating players approve one or more available topics. Tallies stay hidden until all ballots lock; the top total wins and tied leaders have equal random chances. A tie is drawn automatically, then the host begins the round. See design/topic-voting-v1/README.md.

Every round is Full Cast: each player holds a secret role, at every size from 3 to 12. The lineup grows with the room — Imposter and Decoy at three, the Judge at four, the Accomplice and Detective at five, Gamblers for everyone beyond them — and the Judge's double vote and the Decoy's cousin word switch on as the room gets big enough to carry them. Roles are dealt by weighted history, not rotated, so the "no role rotation" rule still holds. See PLAN.md §13.

Session scoring follows the approved design/session-scoring-v1 mockups. The target locks at the first round and final totals remain visible after the target is reached. Highest final score wins, with shared wins for tied leaders. The host starts the next score session explicitly: scores, bets, and pending report deductions reset; connected players, join credentials, topic settings, used-word history, and imposter fairness history stay with the party. See design/session-scoring-v1/IMPLEMENTATION.md for the settled rules and verification.
